How it works

  • Ask for a meeting. Review it. Send.

    Text or email ZipSched who needs to meet, for how long, and when. It checks the calendar and prepares an invitation with the attendees, time, and details.

    Review the suggestion, edit anything you need, and approve it to send from your own connected account.

  • Availability with context

    A calendar entry doesn’t always mean its owner is unavailable. You might keep a colleague’s travel on the calendar for reference, even though you’re free to meet.

    ZipSched tells travel, tentative holds and focus time apart from the meetings you need to attend, and uses that context when it suggests times.

  • Help with scheduling emails

    ZipSched prepares replies to scheduling requests, suggests times, and drafts calendar actions from the thread.

    Review and edit each one with the thread beside it, and draw availability right on the calendar.

  • Adjusting the time without starting over

    When someone cancels or needs to reschedule, ZipSched prepares a new time for your approval.

    You can also see other times that work, and choose to configure automatic rescheduling.
